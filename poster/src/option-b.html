Abstract

Forecasting models trained at one solar plant often lose accuracy at a new site. Our accepted NEECON 2026 paper measured both outcomes: successful transfer within a region and negative transfer across regions. SolarTwin AI is a software digital twin that checks transfer risk, compares forecast models, plans battery and flexible-load actions within hard limits, and records why each action was taken.

Problem Statement & Objectives

New plants have little generation history. A model borrowed from another plant can drift without warning, and an energy schedule built on that forecast inherits the error.

  1. Detect when a transferred forecast should not be trusted.
  2. Decide a safe battery and flexible-load schedule within operating limits.
  3. Explain each decision with the evidence that produced it.

Related Literature

Solar forecasting usually depends on site-specific history. Domain adaptation (DANN, CDAN) aligns source and target features so a model can transfer without target labels; our ISGT Middle East 2025 study reached R² = 0.75 in blind cross-plant transfer [2]. Digital twins let operators test plant and storage decisions in simulation before acting on them.
